The rules in numbers

Distances are in metres, times in minutes of battle time, speeds in metres a minute.

Ground and sight. A body sees enemy troops in the open up to 2500 <!-- vision.RANGE_OPEN_M --> m away, but troops in a wood or village only from 400 <!-- vision.RANGE_INTO_COVER_M --> m; woods and villages block sight more than 50 <!-- world.LOS_BLOCK_DEPTH_M --> m deep. Beyond 1200 <!-- vision.IDENT_ROUGH_M --> m an enemy is only "troops"; within it his arm and his size to the nearest 100 <!-- vision.ROUGH_ROUND --> men are made out, within 500 <!-- vision.IDENT_EXACT_M --> m his exact strength and formation. A brigade remembers an enemy it has lost sight of for 30 <!-- vision.MEMORY_MIN --> minutes. Headquarters sees by the same ranges from where it stands. A routed body sees nothing.

Marching. Speeds of fresh troops, by road and across open country:
- Infantry in column 75 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["inf_column"][0] --> and 60 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["inf_column"][1] -->; in line 40 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["inf_line"][1] -->, skirmish order 50 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["inf_skirmish"][1] -->, square 20 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["inf_square"][1] -->. Bold assaults go at the double, 1.3 <!-- movement.DOUBLE_TIME --> times as fast.
- Cavalry at the walk 125 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["cav_walk"][0] --> and 100 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["cav_walk"][1] -->, the trot 250 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["cav_trot"][0] --> and 200 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["cav_trot"][1] -->, the gallop 350 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["cav_gallop"][1] -->; after 3 <!-- movement.GALLOP_MAX_MIN --> minutes at the gallop it is blown.
- Foot batteries 60 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["art_foot"][0] --> and 40 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["art_foot"][1] -->, horse batteries 100 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["art_horse"][1] -->.
- Ground multiplies speed: wood 0.5 <!-- movement.COVER_MULT["inf"][Cover.WOOD] --> for foot, 0.25 <!-- movement.COVER_MULT["cav"][Cover.WOOD] --> for horse and guns; village 0.5 <!-- movement.COVER_MULT["inf"][Cover.VILLAGE] -->; broken ground and orchards 0.7 <!-- movement.COVER_MULT["inf"][Cover.ROUGH] -->; marsh 0.3 <!-- movement.COVER_MULT["inf"][Cover.MARSH] -->, closed to guns off the road. Water is crossed only at bridges and fords; a ford passes foot and horse at 0.5 <!-- world.FORD_SPEED --> speed and no wheels, unless your situation says otherwise.
- Fatigue runs up to 100 <!-- movement.FATIGUE_MAX -->. It rises each minute by 1 <!-- movement.FATIGUE_DOUBLE_OR_TROT --> at the trot or the double, 3 <!-- movement.FATIGUE_GALLOP_OR_MELEE --> at the gallop or in melee, 0.5 <!-- movement.FATIGUE_MUSKETRY --> while firing muskets; ordinary marching costs nothing; halted and out of the fight a body recovers 1 <!-- movement: -FATIGUE_RECOVER --> a minute. Exhausted troops march and fire at 0.5 <!-- movement: 1 - FATIGUE_MAX / FATIGUE_SLOW_DIV --> of their fresh rate; disorder slows them the same way.
- Formation changes: column to line 3 <!-- movement.CHANGE_MIN[("column", "line")] --> minutes, line to column 2 <!-- movement.CHANGE_MIN[("line", "column")] -->, square 2 <!-- movement.CHANGE_MIN[("*", "square")] -->, skirmish order 2 <!-- movement.CHANGE_MIN[("*", "skirmish")] -->, cavalry 1 <!-- movement.CAV_CHANGE_MIN -->; a foot battery limbers or unlimbers in 3 <!-- movement.CHANGE_MIN[("limbered", "unlimbered")] -->, a horse battery in 2 <!-- movement.HORSE_LIMBER_MIN -->. Turning costs a minute per 90 <!-- movement.FACING_DEG_PER_MIN --> degrees.
- Infantry sent against a known enemy deploy from column into line at 600 <!-- drill.DEPLOY_M[Manner.CAUTIOUS] --> m from him if ordered carefully, 400 <!-- drill.DEPLOY_M[Manner.MEASURED] --> ordinarily, 300 <!-- drill.DEPLOY_M[Manner.BOLD] --> boldly. Told to attack, the line halts to fire at 200 <!-- drill.FIRE_HALT_M[Manner.CAUTIOUS] -->, 120 <!-- drill.FIRE_HALT_M[Manner.MEASURED] -->, 80 <!-- drill.FIRE_HALT_M[Manner.BOLD] --> or 60 <!-- drill.FIRE_HALT_M[Manner.RECKLESS] --> m (carefully, ordinarily, boldly, at all costs) and goes in with the bayonet after 10 <!-- drill.ASSAULT_AFTER_MIN[Manner.MEASURED] -->, 5 <!-- drill.ASSAULT_AFTER_MIN[Manner.BOLD] --> or 0 <!-- drill.ASSAULT_AFTER_MIN[Manner.RECKLESS] --> minutes of fire, sooner if the enemy falls into heavy disorder; a careful attack never closes. Cavalry told to charge trots from 800 <!-- drill.CAV_TROT_M --> m and gallops the last 250 <!-- drill.CAV_GALLOP_M -->; advancing horse not told to halt or avoid a fight charges any enemy within 600 <!-- drill.CAV_ENGAGE_M --> m. Guns sent against an enemy unlimber within 800 <!-- drill.BOMBARD_STANDOFF_M --> m of him.

Fire. Musketry reaches 250 <!-- combat.MUSKET_RANGE_M --> m. Each musket in line hits 0.015 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[1][1] --> men a minute at 50 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[1][0] --> m, 0.008 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[2][1] --> at 100 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[2][0] -->, 0.0008 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[5][1] --> at 250 <!-- combat.HIT_RATE[5][0] -->, more with quality: 700 <!-- input --> fresh men of quality 3 <!-- input --> kill about 6.7 <!-- combat: 700 * interp(HIT_RATE, 100) * quality_factor(3) --> a minute at 100 <!-- input --> m. A column fires with 0.17 <!-- combat.FIRING_FRACTION[UnitState.COLUMN] --> of its men, a square 0.25 <!-- combat.FIRING_FRACTION[UnitState.SQUARE] -->. A line fires only within 60 <!-- combat.FIRE_ARC_DEG --> degrees of its front, never through friends, and not while moving. Columns take 1.5 <!-- combat.TARGET_STATE_MUSKET[UnitState.COLUMN] --> times the musketry, skirmishers 0.5 <!-- combat.TARGET_STATE_MUSKET[UnitState.SKIRMISH] -->; musketry into a flank counts 1.5 <!-- combat.ASPECT_FACTOR["flank"] -->, into the rear 2 <!-- combat.ASPECT_FACTOR["rear"] -->. Troops in a wood or village take 0.5 <!-- combat.COVER_FACTOR[Cover.WOOD] --> of all fire, on broken ground 0.8 <!-- combat.COVER_FACTOR[Cover.ROUGH] -->.

Guns. Roundshot reaches 1400 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT_RANGE_M[12] --> m from a 12 <!-- input -->-pounder, 1000 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT_RANGE_M[6] --> from a 6 <!-- input -->. A gun kills 1 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT[0][1] --> man a minute out to 300 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT[0][0] --> m, 0.6 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT[1][1] --> at 600 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT[1][0] -->, 0.25 <!-- combat.ROUNDSHOT_AT_MAX --> at extreme range; canister, within 400 <!-- combat.CANISTER_RANGE_M --> m, kills 3 <!-- combat.CANISTER[1][1] --> at 200 <!-- combat.CANISTER[1][0] --> and 5 <!-- combat.CANISTER[0][1] --> at 100 <!-- combat.CANISTER[0][0] -->. Columns suffer 2 <!-- combat.ARTY_TARGET_STATE[UnitState.COLUMN] --> times, squares 2.5 <!-- combat.ARTY_TARGET_STATE[UnitState.SQUARE] -->, skirmishers 0.4 <!-- combat.ARTY_TARGET_STATE[UnitState.SKIRMISH] -->, batteries 0.5 <!-- combat.ARTY_TARGET_STATE[UnitState.UNLIMBERED] -->. Battalion guns fire only while their battalion stands in line, roundshot to 600 <!-- combat.BN_GUN_RANGE_M --> m and canister to 300 <!-- combat.BN_CANISTER_RANGE_M -->, at 0.5 <!-- combat: BN_ROUNDSHOT[0][1] / ROUNDSHOT[0][1] --> a battery gun's effect, and are lost when it is broken hand to hand.

Ammunition. Every body starts with 100 <!-- types.Unit.ammo -->; musketry spends 2 <!-- combat.AMMO_MUSKETRY --> a minute, guns 1 <!-- combat.AMMO_ARTILLERY -->. Below 20 <!-- combat.AMMO_LOW --> fire halves; with none it stops. Nothing is resupplied.

Shock. A body's weight is its men times its quality times its morale times a factor for its formation; the attacker's falls with fatigue, to 0.33 <!-- combat: 1 - 100 / FATIGUE_SHOCK_DIV --> when exhausted, and the umpire may weigh an attack by 0.7 <!-- combat.UMPIRE_RANGE[0] --> to 1.4 <!-- combat.UMPIRE_RANGE[1] --> for its situation. Attacker: horse at the gallop 3 <!-- combat.ATTACK_STATE["gallop"] -->, trot 2 <!-- combat.ATTACK_STATE["trot"] -->; foot in column 1.5 <!-- combat.INF_ATTACK_STATE[UnitState.COLUMN] -->, line 1 <!-- combat.INF_ATTACK_STATE[UnitState.LINE] -->. Defender: square against horse 4 <!-- combat.DEF_SQUARE_VS_CAV -->; steady line against horse 3 <!-- combat.DEF_LINE_VS_CAV_STEADY -->, flanked or disordered 0.5 <!-- combat.DEF_LINE_VS_CAV_SHAKEN -->; line against foot 1.2 <!-- combat.DEF_LINE_VS_INF -->; foot in wood or village 2 <!-- combat.DEF_COVER -->; column 1 <!-- combat.DEF_COLUMN -->; halted horse 0.7 <!-- combat.DEF_CAV_HALTED -->; guns 0.5 <!-- combat.DEF_GUNS_UNLIMBERED -->, limbered 0.2 <!-- combat.DEF_GUNS_LIMBERED -->; anyone else struck in flank or rear 0.5 <!-- combat.DEF_FLANKED_MULT --> of itself. A defender who can fire gets up to two volleys at the attacker coming in. Attacker over 1.5 <!-- combat.BREAK_RATIO --> times the defender's weight: the defender breaks. Under 0.67 <!-- combat.REPULSE_RATIO -->: the attacker is repulsed, loses 0.06 <!-- combat.REPULSE_LOSS --> of his men and falls back 100 <!-- combat.REPULSE_BACK_M --> m. Between: hand to hand for 3 <!-- combat.MELEE_MIN --> minutes, each losing 0.03 <!-- combat.MELEE_LOSS_PER_MIN --> of its men a minute, and the lower morale breaks. Horse meeting horse at the charge both strike; either may break.

Squares. Infantry form square of their own accord when enemy horse is seen within 400 <!-- drill.SQUARE_CAV_M --> m, none of yours is within 600 <!-- drill.SQUARE_COVER_M -->, and a flank lacks a formed friend within 150 <!-- drill.SQUARE_FLANK_M --> m; a line with both flanks covered stays in line, troops in wood or village stay in it. They leave square when no enemy horse has been within 600 <!-- drill.SQUARE_CLEAR_M --> m for 3 <!-- drill.SQUARE_CLEAR_MIN --> minutes. Horse charging a steady square pulls up at 100 <!-- combat.SQUARE_RECOGNISE_M --> m, takes its volley, falls back, and does not charge it again for 15 <!-- command.PULLUP_BAR_MIN --> minutes, nor while it stays in square.

Steadiness. Morale starts at 60 <!-- morale.START_MORALE_BASE --> plus 8 <!-- morale.START_MORALE_PER_QUALITY --> per point of quality and falls 1.5 <!-- morale.MORALE_PER_PCT_LOSS --> for each per cent of the body's starting men lost; below 25 <!-- morale.ROUT_MORALE --> it breaks. Losses taken quickly break quality 2 <!-- input --> at 34 <!-- morale: (START_MORALE_BASE + 2 * START_MORALE_PER_QUALITY - ROUT_MORALE) / MORALE_PER_PCT_LOSS --> per cent, quality 3 <!-- input --> at 39 <!-- morale: (START_MORALE_BASE + 3 * START_MORALE_PER_QUALITY - ROUT_MORALE) / MORALE_PER_PCT_LOSS -->, quality 5 <!-- input --> at 50 <!-- morale: (START_MORALE_BASE + 5 * START_MORALE_PER_QUALITY - ROUT_MORALE) / MORALE_PER_PCT_LOSS -->. A body breaking costs friends within 300 <!-- morale.NEIGHBOUR_ROUT_M --> m 5 <!-- morale.NEIGHBOUR_ROUT_MORALE --> morale. Out of the fight and taking no losses, a body regains 0.2 <!-- morale.MORALE_RECOVER --> a minute, never above its start. Below 0.1 <!-- morale.DESTROYED_FRACTION --> of its starting men a body is destroyed.

Rout and rally. A broken body flees until no enemy is within 1000 <!-- drill.ROUT_FLEE_CLEAR_M --> m, and regains 0.5 <!-- morale.ROUTED_RECOVER --> morale a minute while none is within 800 <!-- morale.ROUTED_SAFE_M --> m, 2 <!-- morale.RALLY_RECOVER --> under an order to rally. It rallies when no enemy is within 600 <!-- morale.RALLY_CLEAR_M --> m, its clock has run, and its morale has reached 55 <!-- morale.SELF_RALLY_MORALE -->, or 45 <!-- morale.RALLY_MORALE --> under the order: from the breaking point at least 60 <!-- morale: (SELF_RALLY_MORALE - ROUT_MORALE) / ROUTED_RECOVER --> minutes alone, 10 <!-- morale: (RALLY_MORALE - ROUT_MORALE) / RALLY_RECOVER --> with the order. It returns in column and disorder to its formation's task. The clocks, minutes before a body may rally and before it may charge or assault again: repulsed 4 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["repulse"][0] --> and 6 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["repulse"][1] -->; broken by fire or a lost melee 6 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["defeat"][0] --> and 12 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["defeat"][1] -->; broken at the first shock 10 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["complete"][0] --> and 20 <!-- morale.RECOVERY_MIN["complete"][1] -->. Horse re-forms for 5 <!-- drill.REFORM_MIN --> minutes after any charge.

Command. Aides ride 400 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["courier_road"][0] --> m a minute by road, 250 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["courier_road"][1] --> across country, crossing water only at bridges and fords; with no way across the order comes back. An order leaves when you write it and acts when its aide arrives; an order moving headquarters acts at once, and headquarters rides straight at 200 <!-- movement.BASE_SPEED["hq"][0] --> m a minute. A brigade reports on first contact, on being engaged, on reaching its objective, when a body breaks, when its losses pass 25 <!-- command: REPORT_LOSS_FRACTION * 100 --> per cent, and otherwise every 30 <!-- command.REPORT_EVERY_MIN --> minutes; reports ride back at the aides' pace.

The clock. You are consulted every 15 <!-- play.CADENCE_MIN --> minutes and at once on a report of contact, of a body broken, or a staff question, but not twice within 5 <!-- play.TRIGGER_GAP_MIN --> minutes. Casualties are whole men, drawn by lot around the expected figure; all else follows these rules exactly.
