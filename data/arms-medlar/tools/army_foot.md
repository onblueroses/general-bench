You command the {army}, an army strong in foot: some thirteen thousand two hundred men and fifty-six guns in eleven formations. Its foot is eight brigades of two battalions of seven hundred men, each with two battalion guns: the Grenadier brigade under {G}, picked men, quality 5 on a scale of 1 to 5; the First to the Fifth brigades under {L1}, {L2}, {L3}, {L4} and {L5}, steady regiments of the line, quality 3; the Sixth and Seventh brigades under {N1} and {N2}, new-raised regiments, quality 2. Its horse is two brigades of two regiments of four hundred: the Dragoon brigade under {DR}, quality 3; the Hussar brigade under {HU}, light horse, quality 3. The Reserve artillery under {AR} is three batteries of eight twelve-pounders, quality 3. Your headquarters stands behind the centre.

The army is rested and fed. It stands drawn up across the Post road in two lines, its first line three and a half kilometres from Medlar Village: the First, Second, Third and Fourth brigades from left to right, the Hussar brigade on the left wing and the Dragoon brigade on the right; behind them the second line, the Sixth, Fifth and Seventh brigades and on the right the Grenadier brigade in column; behind the centre the Reserve artillery limbered.

The enemy marches on Medlar Village from the other side of the plain. His army is unlike yours: the {enemy_army}, strong in horse, some eleven thousand two hundred men and thirty-six guns in eleven formations. Five brigades of foot, each of two battalions of seven hundred with two battalion guns apiece: a Grenadier brigade, quality 5, three brigades of the line, quality 3, and one of new-raised regiments, quality 2. Five brigades of horse, each of two regiments of four hundred: a Cuirassier brigade, heavy horse, quality 4, two Dragoon brigades and two Hussar brigades, quality 3. A Horse artillery of two batteries of eight six-pounders, quality 3. How he has drawn them up and what he intends is not known.
