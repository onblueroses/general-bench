You command the {army}, an army strong in horse: some eleven thousand two hundred men and thirty-six guns in eleven formations. Its foot is five brigades of two battalions of seven hundred men, each with two battalion guns: the Grenadier brigade under {G}, picked men, quality 5 on a scale of 1 to 5; the First, Second and Third brigades under {L1}, {L2} and {L3}, steady regiments of the line, quality 3; the Fourth brigade under {N1}, new-raised regiments, quality 2. Its horse is five brigades of two regiments of four hundred: the Cuirassier brigade under {CU}, heavy horse, quality 4; the First and Second Dragoon brigades under {DR1} and {DR2}, quality 3; the First and Second Hussar brigades under {HU1} and {HU2}, light horse, quality 3. The Horse artillery under {AR} is two batteries of eight six-pounders, quality 3. Your headquarters stands behind the centre.

The army is rested and fed. It stands drawn up across the Post road, its first line three and a half kilometres from Medlar Village: the First, Second and Third brigades from left to right, the First Hussar brigade on the left wing and the Second on the right; behind them the second line, the Fourth brigade on the left and the Grenadier brigade in column on the right, the two Dragoon brigades behind the wings; behind the centre the Cuirassier brigade and the Horse artillery limbered.

The enemy marches on Medlar Village from the other side of the plain. His army is unlike yours: the {enemy_army}, strong in foot, some thirteen thousand two hundred men and fifty-six guns in eleven formations. Eight brigades of foot, each of two battalions of seven hundred with two battalion guns apiece: a Grenadier brigade, quality 5, five brigades of the line, quality 3, and two of new-raised regiments, quality 2. Two brigades of horse, each of two regiments of four hundred: a Dragoon brigade and a Hussar brigade, quality 3. A Reserve artillery of three batteries of eight twelve-pounders, quality 3. How he has drawn them up and what he intends is not known.
