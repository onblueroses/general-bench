# Medlar Village: objectives

Each side's mission in its commander's words, the same for both sides (tools/objectives_template.md); the generals read it, with the verdict, in players.md. Designed battle: docs/battles/arms.md.

## Horse: General Ashdown

Take and hold the crossroads at Medlar Village. The enemy marches on it from the other side under the
same orders. The object is to win the day; a draw is worth half a win to each side.

The verdict, stated to the general as it will be ruled (read by rule, not by an umpire's
opinion):

1. An army that breaks loses. Broken: more than 40% of its men are in routed or destroyed
   bodies and stay so for 30 minutes together; if rallies bring the share under 40% within
   the half hour, the count starts again. The battle ends at that hour. If both armies are
   over 40% then, the less broken army wins, unless the two shares are within 10 points of
   each other (about a brigade of foot), when it is drawn.
2. Otherwise the side in possession of the village at the end hour wins. Possession is read
   within musket range (250 m) of the crossroads: the side with formed foot or guns there
   (steady or shaken, not in flight or routed) while the enemy has none; if neither has foot
   or guns there, the side with formed cavalry there while the enemy has none. Cavalry holds
   an empty village and holds it against cavalry, never against formed foot or guns, for in
   this game horse fire no volleys and dismount no dragoons.
3. Contested at the end hour: play goes on, the village is read again every quarter hour, the
   staff tell the general each reading, and the first clear possession wins. Still contested
   at the play-on bound: a draw, unless one army is then over 40% broken and the other is
   not, in which case the broken army loses.
4. Nothing else counts. Casualties, ground elsewhere and the condition of the army for
   tomorrow decide nothing (they are recorded as the margin, for reading, never for the
   verdict).

The clock: the battle starts at 09:00; the end hour is 16:00; play-on until 18:00 at most.

## Umpire (the narrative only)

Under this pack the umpire narrates and does not rule. The verdict is mechanical: mission.json is the ordinal verdict above (kriegsspiel.mission.OrdinalJudge), which ends the battle on a break and reads possession of Medlar Village within musket range of the crossroads at 16:00 and in the play-on. The umpire's own verdict is kept beside it as a health number, never as the result. The two armies differ: the White army (horse) is strong in cavalry, the Green army (foot) in infantry and guns. Name in the account Medlar Village (the crossroads), the Post road, the Salt road, Quince Farm, Damson Farm, Bullace Farm, Pippin Farm and the four exits, West, East, North and South.
